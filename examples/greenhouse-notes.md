# Paired greenhouse observations

This fictional source illustrates how a graph can cite an observation that a reader can inspect.

| Bed | Soil moisture by volume | Air temperature | Seedlings at noon |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| East | 12% | 29 °C | Wilted |
| West | 26% | 29 °C | Upright |

The measurements cover one point in time. A follow-up after watering would show whether the wilted seedlings recovered.
